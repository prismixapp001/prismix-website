PRISMIX Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

PRISMIX ("we", "our", or "the app") is an LGBTQ+ movie information and discovery platform. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use PRISMIX.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, PRISMIX may collect information such as your email address and account information when you create or use an account.

If you contact support, report a problem, or send feedback, we may collect the information you provide, including your email address and the contents of your request.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to provide and maintain PRISMIX, authenticate users, respond to support requests, improve the app, maintain security, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Firebase and Third-Party Services

PRISMIX may use third-party services such as Google Firebase for authentication and database services. These services may process information as necessary to provide their functions.

4. Advertising

PRISMIX may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Advertising providers may process device or advertising-related information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your PRISMIX account and associated user data by visiting our Account Deletion page.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or when retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or compliance purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

PRISMIX is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
