PRISMIX Account Deletion

If you want to permanently delete your PRISMIX account and associated user data, you can submit an account deletion request without reinstalling or opening the app.

How to request account deletion

Send an email from the email address associated with your PRISMIX account to:

prismix.app@gmail.com

Use the subject line PRISMIX Account Deletion Request and include the email address associated with your PRISMIX account.

Deletion: We will process the request and delete the account and associated user data that we are required and able to delete. Certain information may be retained only when necessary for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or compliance purposes.

For questions about account deletion or privacy, contact prismix.app@gmail.com.
